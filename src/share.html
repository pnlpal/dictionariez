A note from the developer behind

I'm the indie developer behind . For more than ten years I've been building it, mostly in my spare time, with a lot of help from people like you.

For months I've been working on something I've wanted to give you for a long time: Puffins, Dictionariez for your phone. Today I'm really excited to say it's finally here, on both Android and iPhone.

Since the very beginning I've promised the core would always be free, and Puffins keeps that promise: every dictionary, every lookup, free, with no ads and no tracking.

The only paid part is AI Lookup. Each AI answer costs me real money in AI provider fees and cloud servers, so after a free trial it's part of Pro. That's what keeps everything else free for everyone.

If you give it a try, I'd love to hear what you think. Your feedback shaped Dictionariez, and it will shape Puffins too.

— River pnl.dev
